Distance Between Two Points

Question: Find the exact distance and approximate to three decimal places.
(−7, −9)
(3, −15)
Progress
0 / 3 warmups
01   What is this asking?

The distance formula tells you the straight-line distance between any two points on a graph. It comes directly from the Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c².

The formula
d = √[ (x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)² ]

Why it works: The horizontal gap (x₂−x₁) and vertical gap (y₂−y₁) form the two legs of a right triangle. The distance is the hypotenuse — so you square both legs, add, then square root.

⚡ Exam trick — negatives disappear when you square
(x₂ − x₁) might be negative, but you square it — so it becomes positive anyway. You never need to worry about the sign of the differences. Just subtract, then square.
Rounding to 3 Decimal Places
the exam asks for "nearest thousandth"
Decimal Rounding
— thousandths = 3rd decimal place
Places
Ones . Tenths Hundredths Thousandths Ten-thousandths…
Example: 11.6618… — the 4th digit after the dot tells you whether to round up or keep.
Rule
Look at the 4th decimal digit. If it's 5 or more → round UP the 3rd digit. If it's 4 or less → keep the 3rd digit as-is (round down).
Trick: cover everything after the 3rd decimal place. Look at the one digit you're about to cut. ≥ 5 → bump up. < 5 → leave it.

Examples:

11.6618… → 4th digit is 8 (≥5) → round up 3rd digit 11.662 ✓
3.1415… → 4th digit is 5 (≥5) → round up 3.142 ✓
2.4494… → 4th digit is 4 (<5) → keep 2.449 ✓
7.9987… → 4th digit is 7 (≥5) → round up → 7.999 ✓

04   Solve it
exact answer first, then approximate
Part A
Exact distance
1
Label the points
(x₁, y₁) = (−7, −9)    (x₂, y₂) = (3, −15)
2
Find the differences
x₂ − x₁ = 3 − (−7) = 3 + 7 = 10
y₂ − y₁ = −15 − (−9) = −15 + 9 = −6
3
Square both differences
10² = 100
(−6)² = 36   ← negative squared = positive
4
Add and take square root
d = √(100 + 36) = √136
5
Simplify √136
Factor 136: 136 = 4 × 34. Since 4 is a perfect square:
√136 = √(4 × 34) = √4 × √34 = 2√34
Answer — Part A (Exact)
d = 2√34
√136 simplified
Factor out perfect square 4
Part B
Approximate to 3 decimal places
6
Calculate 2√34 on a calculator
√34 ≈ 5.8309518…
2 × 5.8309518… = 11.6619038…
7
Round to 3 decimal places
Look at the 4th decimal: 11.6619038 — 9 ≥ 5 → round up 3rd digit:
11.6619… ≈ 11.662
Answer — Part B (Approximate)
d ≈ 11.662
4th decimal was 9 → rounded up
