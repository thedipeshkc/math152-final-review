① Plain English
what is this question actually asking?

"Express in terms of sums and differences" means: break this one big log apart into several simpler logs that are added or subtracted. You're going in the opposite direction from combining — you're expanding or splitting.


The expression log √(b⁹c) looks complicated, but you'll use just 3 rules, applied one at a time:


Step 1 — Convert the square root to a power: √ = raise to the ½.
Step 2 — Use the power rule to pull the ½ out front.
Step 3 — Use the product rule to split log(b⁹ · c) into two logs.
Step 4 — Use the power rule again to pull the 9 out of log(b⁹).


The final answer will be two terms: one for b and one for c, both multiplied by fractions. No square roots, no products left — just clean simple logs.

③ Cheat Sheet
all three log rules you need — screenshot this
Log Rules
— expanding a logarithm uses these three
Power Rule
log(Mᵖ) = p · log M
Exponent moves out front as a multiplier. Works for any base.
Product Rule
log(M · N) = log M + log N
Multiplication inside the log → addition outside.
Quotient Rule
log(M / N) = log M − log N
Division inside the log → subtraction outside. (Not needed here but common on exams.)
√ = ½ power
√(X) = X^(1/2) · ∛(X) = X^(1/3) · ⁴√(X) = X^(1/4)
Always convert roots to fractional exponents before applying log rules.
Order of attack for expanding:
1️⃣ Convert all roots to fractional powers  →  2️⃣ Pull the outer power out front (power rule)  →  3️⃣ Split products/quotients (product/quotient rule)  →  4️⃣ Pull any remaining inner powers out front again

④ Step-by-Step Solution
expanding log √(b⁹c) completely
Expand
log √(b⁹c) → sums and differences of logs
1
Convert the square root to a fractional exponent
√(anything) = (anything)^(1/2). Rewrite the whole expression under the root.
log √(b⁹c) = log(b⁹c)^(1/2)
2
Apply the Power Rule — bring ½ out front
log(Aᵖ) = p · log A. Here A = b⁹c and p = 1/2.
= ½ · log(b⁹c)
3
Apply the Product Rule — split the product inside
log(M · N) = log M + log N. Split b⁹ and c apart.
= ½ · [log(b⁹) + log c]
4
Apply the Power Rule again — bring the 9 out front
log(b⁹) = 9 · log b. Pull the exponent 9 to the front.
= ½ · [9 log b + log c]
5
Distribute the ½ — multiply through
½ × 9 = 9/2  ·  ½ × 1 = 1/2
= (9/2) log b + (1/2) log c
Final Answer
(9/2) log b + (1/2) log c
power rule used twice
product rule used once
no roots remain in answer
✓ Numerical Check (b = 10, c = 100)
Original: log √(10⁹ · 100) = log √(10⁹ · 10²) = log √(10¹¹) = log(10^5.5) = 5.5
Our answer: (9/2)·log 10 + (1/2)·log 100 = 4.5·1 + 0.5·2 = 4.5 + 1 = 5.5 ✓
